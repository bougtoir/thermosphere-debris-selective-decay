## Input-data ledger

The study uses **no downloaded observational data**. Every input is either a
configuration constant (`config/default.yaml`) or generated deterministically
from a pinned model implementation, so the "raw data" of this study is the
model output persisted under version control:

| artifact | generator | persisted at | nature |
|---|---|---|---|
| `data/processed/density_lookup.csv` | `src/atmosphere/lookup.py` -> `nrlmsise00.msise_model` (gtd7), epoch 2020-06-21 12:00 UTC, log-mean over lat {-80,-40,0,40,80} x lon {-180,-90,0,90} x LST {0,6,12,18} h, 100-900 km at 1 km | repository (committed) | model output, deterministic |
| `data/processed/cases.csv`, `population.csv` | `scripts/phase04_populations.py` with seed from `config/default.yaml` | repository (committed) | synthetic, deterministic |
| solar/geomagnetic regimes (low/moderate/high/storm F10.7, Ap) | `config/default.yaml` | repository (committed) | literature-typical constants, cited in manuscript |

No external URL, API, or third-party dataset is fetched at any point in the
pipeline, so no download ledger, checksum-of-origin, or licence-transfer record
applies. Checksums of the generated inputs are emitted by the submission
packager (`MANIFEST.md`) and are re-verified in Phase 18.

## Known defects carried into the revision

1. Sobol' indices not converged; `S1 > ST` for `delta_max` (Phase 2).
2. Selectivity ratios dominated by Gaussian-tail numerical zeros (Phase 3).
3. "41%" described in places as a probability although the grid is a design
   survey with no sampling distribution (Phase 4).
4. Finite-horizon `inf`/1826.5 d values not labelled as right-censored
   (Phase 7).
5. Scaling-collapse constant stated inconsistently in prose
   (`dv*B/(rho*delta*v*T)` in the handoff, `v^2` in the code) and presented as
   validation rather than internal consistency (Phase 11).
6. Idealized tracking bound framing not uniformly explicit across all
   occurrences (Phase 5).

## Preservation policy

`pre-revision-checkpoint` pins the complete frozen tree, including every CSV,
figure and document quoted above. Revision work overwrites regenerated outputs
in place on the revision branch; the frozen values remain recoverable via
`git show pre-revision-checkpoint:<path>` and are quoted verbatim here for
side-by-side comparison in the audit reports.
