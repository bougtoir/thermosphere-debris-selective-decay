Implication for the ASR rejection: the baseline manuscript cited 18 references,
none of the physical-model response studies above (Liu 2015, Deng 2008, Sutton
2009, Lühr 2004, Carlson 2012, Clemmons 2008). Propositions 1, 2, 4, 5 and 12
were therefore presented without connecting them to work that already
establishes them, and propositions 6–9 (the only candidates for novelty) were
argued only with prescribed fields. Both positioning and physical support were
deficient.
