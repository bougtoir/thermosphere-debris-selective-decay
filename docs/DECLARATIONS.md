## Declaration of generative AI and AI-assisted technologies

During the preparation of this work the authors used an AI coding and
research assistant (Devin, Cognition AI) to implement the simulation
and analysis code, to run the reproducibility and audit pipeline, and
to draft text. All model design decisions, scientific interpretation
and the final content were reviewed and approved by the authors, who
take full responsibility for the content of the publication. No
generative AI was used to create or alter any figure, image or data:
every figure is produced directly from the analysis code from the
result CSVs in this repository.

## Ethics and dual-use statement

No human or animal subjects are involved. No operational spacecraft is
named or targeted; all target and protected objects are synthetic and
defined in `data/processed/cases.csv`. The study reports a feasibility
boundary and explicitly does not propose, design, or evaluate hardware,
deployment procedures, or targeting methods. The idealized tracking
configuration is a mathematical upper bound used to close the
feasibility argument, not a proposed capability.
