# Acta final delta — claim calibration audit (Gates 6–7)

Scope: every sentence in the title, abstract, introduction, results, discussion, conclusions, highlights, captions
and cover letter that attributes the GITM-versus-static-patch difference to a mechanism, or that overstates
robustness, plus all "preregistration" terminology. Text is generated by `scripts/build_physical_manuscript.py`;
numbers are unchanged unless stated.

What the evidence supports: under the tested forcing, background and orbits, the dynamically evolving GITM density
field gives much lower cumulative target/non-target drag selectivity than static Gaussian patches flown along the
same orbits (all 18 Fig. 4 comparator cells at 400/500 km exceed GITM by ≥ 1.52×; `results/tables/acta_semantic_checks.csv`).
The static patches differ from GITM in time evolution, spatial spread, peak location, shape, persistence, matching
choice and orbital exposure at once, and no transport budget was computed, so the difference cannot be assigned to
one mechanism. Spreading itself is a model diagnostic (participation area, radius of gyration, Fig. 2) and may be
stated as such.
